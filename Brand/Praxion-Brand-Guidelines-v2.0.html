01 — Foundation

Our story.

Praxion is a Human Resource Operating System — an HROS — built from the ground up for organizations that want HR to stop being a bottleneck and start being a multiplier.

Mission

We give HR teams a single source of truth for people, and the execution layer to act on it — so every decision about a person is fast, fair, and traceable.

Vision

A workplace where every employee experience is intentional, every compliance requirement is met by default, and every HR leader finally has the intelligence to see around corners.

Execution

Work gets done.

Workflows complete. Approvals close. Payroll runs. HR stops being where requests go to die.

Intelligence

Insight by default.

Every interaction generates data. Every dashboard answers a real question. You see the org before the org sees itself.

Flow

Nothing stutters.

Employee to manager to HR to payroll to ledger — one unbroken motion. No re-keying. No context loss. No handoff tax.

02 — Foundation

How we position.

Praxion is not a suite of HR apps. It is an operating system for HR — one data model, thirteen modules, one voice across every touchpoint.

Positioning Statement

For HR leaders who are tired of stitching together disconnected tools, Praxion is the unified HR operating system that gives them an end-to-end platform — from hire to retire, payroll to performance — built on one model and one voice, so HR finally operates at the speed of the business.

We are
  • → An operating system, not a tool.
  • → Built on one model — people, positions, policies.
  • → Opinionated about process, flexible about policy.
  • → Designed for mid-market and enterprise, not SMB.
  • → Regionally-aware: MENA first, global-ready.
We are not
  • → A payroll point solution.
  • → An applicant tracking system.
  • → A bolt-on survey tool.
  • → A generic workflow engine with HR labels.
  • → The cheapest option — we are the right one.

03 — Identity

The Praxion logo.

A three-dimensional cube anchored by four colored vertices. It reads as structure with motion — an operating system with personality. The amber dot at the bottom-left is deliberate: it breaks the cool-palette symmetry and signals the brand's willingness to make a sharp call when it matters.

Construction

The cube

An isometric hex-footprint cube with three visible faces. The top face carries illumination (white→ice-blue gradient), the left face carries cool shadow, and the right face is teal-infused — a quiet way to double-down on our growth color without adding another accent.

The dots

Seven vertex dots, sized in proportion to the cube. Four are Navy; the other three carry our three brand accents — Signal Blue (top), Ion Teal (bottom-right), and Amber (bottom-left). The amber dot is the only warm note in the mark; it is doing load-bearing work and must never be substituted.

The shadow

A stacked-ellipse ground shadow sits the mark in space. It is baked into every file; do not add a second shadow in Photoshop or Figma.

The wordmark

Set in Inter ExtraBold with custom spacing. The cube rests to the left, the wordmark to the right, with the tagline EXECUTION · INTELLIGENCE · FLOW sitting beneath a short gradient bar (Ion → Signal).

04 — Identity

Clear space & minimum sizes.

The logo needs room to breathe. Protect it with an invisible margin equal to the height of the cube on all four sides. Inside that boundary, nothing — no text, no imagery, no edge.

The rule

X = cube height

Measure the cube at its tallest point (top vertex to bottom vertex). That distance is X. Every side of the logo must have at least X of empty space before anything else appears.

Applies to

Page edges · other logos · text · photography · background imagery · patterns. If you aren't sure, double it.

Minimum sizes.

Below these sizes the mark loses legibility. If you need to go smaller, switch to the icon-only variant.

Digital
Full lockupmin 120 px wide
Icon onlymin 24 px
Print
Full lockupmin 30 mm wide

05 — Identity

Logo misuse.

The approved files carry every tweak we need. Modifying them — even in small ways — breaks recognition across the system. Never do any of the following.

✗Don't place the color lockup on a non-approved background color.
✗Don't rotate, skew, or tilt the logo.
✗Don't stretch or re-proportion the lockup.
✗Don't recolor. The palette is fixed.
✗Don't place the color logo over busy photography — use the inverse variant.
✗Don't add glows, shadows, strokes, or effects.

Pairing rules.

Do
  • → Inter for everything that labels, titles, or commands.
  • → DM Sans for everything that a user reads in-line.
  • → JetBrains Mono for anything precise (IDs, dates, amounts, code).
  • → Pair bold heading with regular body — never bold + bold.
  • → Negative tracking on display (-.02 to -.04em).
Don't
  • → Don't substitute with a different sans-serif ("just use Roboto").
  • → Don't set body text in Inter — it's built for UI, not reading.
  • → Don't set paragraphs in mono.
  • → Don't use italics on display headings.
  • → Don't go below 13px for any body copy.

09 — System

Imagery.

We show real workplaces and real moments. Natural light, candid composition, an emphasis on the human gesture — a hand reaching for a keyboard, a manager leaning across a desk — over staged heroics. The camera is a quiet observer, not the star.

Do photograph
  • → Diverse, multi-generational teams in their actual work environments
  • → Gestures and micro-moments, not posed group shots
  • → Natural daylight; avoid harsh fluorescent or cinematic gel lighting
  • → MENA-region offices, factories, clinics, and classrooms (we're regional first)
  • → Technology in use, never as hero object
Avoid
  • → Stock imagery of people "celebrating" around laptops
  • → Handshake-in-suits clichés
  • → Ethnic or gender monocultures
  • → Heavy filters, vintage fades, or Instagram presets
  • → Imagery of tools that aren't ours (other dashboards, other logos)

Treatments.

Two approved post-processing treatments — anything else must be approved by Brand.

Natural

Unfiltered, true color. Default for editorial and case studies.

Navy duotone

Black→Navy for moody hero imagery over which we overlay the inverse logo or white copy.

Ion gradient tint

Teal→Signal soft tint for growth / analytics content. Used sparingly, mostly in marketing.

10 — System

Illustration.

Our illustration style extends the cube. Geometric. Isometric where it helps. Flat fills with occasional gradients for face-shading. No fleshy characters, no Corporate Memphis, no hand-drawn squiggles.

Geometric · isometric

Primary style. Built from cube-derived forms.

Flat data-viz style

For dashboards, onboarding, empty states.

Rule of thumb

Every illustration should look like it could tile next to the cube logo without feeling out of place. If it couldn't, it isn't on-brand.

11 — Voice

Voice & tone.

Praxion sounds like a trusted operator: calm, specific, slightly dry, never hype. We explain clearly, we don't oversell, and we respect that the reader is probably mid-shift.

Calm

No alarm language. Even when something breaks, we describe what happened and what to do — then stop.

Specific

Numbers over adjectives. "Payroll ran in 4 minutes" beats "fast payroll." Always.

Dry

One degree drier than most SaaS. No exclamation points. No "rockstar." No "magic."

Respectful

We assume the reader is busy and capable. No hand-holding, no corporate jargon, no "touchbase."

Voice in practice.

✓ Praxion sounds like

"Payroll for April has been approved. 412 employees. EGP 2.4M net. You can review the audit trail before finalizing."

✗ Not

"🎉 Yay! Your April payroll is ready to go! We've made it super easy to review — just hit the button below!"

✓ Praxion sounds like

"Three probation reviews are overdue. The longest has been open 9 days. Assigning them to managers will send reminders automatically."

✗ Not

"Uh-oh! Some probation reviews need your attention — don't forget to complete them to keep everyone happy!"

✓ Praxion sounds like

"We couldn't sync today's attendance because the biometric device at Gate 3 is offline. Manual entry is available while we retry."

✗ Not

"Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later or contact support if the issue persists."

Words we use / don't use.

Use
  • operate, run, manage, review, approve, sync, flow
  • HR team · HR leader · HR ops · people team
  • workforce · employees · managers · administrators
  • platform · operating system · module · workflow
  • exact nouns: "payroll cycle" not "payroll thing"
Avoid
  • empower, unleash, supercharge, skyrocket
  • best-in-class, world-class, game-changing
  • revolutionize, transform, reimagine
  • "just", "simply", "easy" (they're rarely true)
  • rockstar, ninja, guru, wizard — ever

Accessibility.

The brand is only as strong as its legibility. Every color combination we use publicly meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for body text (4.5:1) or large display text (3.0:1). The combinations below are pre-approved.

Focus states

Always visible. 3px Signal-blue outline with 2px offset. Never remove. Never replace with color change alone.

Motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Disable parallax, auto-playing gradients, and non-essential transitions.

Typography

Minimum 13px for any UI body copy. Minimum 15px for long-form reading content.

15 — Summary

Do & Don't.

Every rule from the book above, condensed. If you're about to use the brand in a way that isn't covered here, ask Brand first.

Do
  • Use the approved v2 logo files from /Brand/logos/v2-production/
  • Match the variant to the background (inverse on dark, mono for single-color)
  • Preserve clear space (X = cube height on all four sides)
  • Let Navy dominate (60%), one accent support (30%), one accent lift (10%)
  • Pair Inter headings with DM Sans body
  • Use JetBrains Mono for numbers, amounts, dates, IDs
  • Write in calm, specific, dry sentences
  • Use outline-style 2px-stroke Lucide icons
  • Check every text/background combination for WCAG AA contrast
Don't
  • Don't edit files inside v2-production/ — create v3 if change is needed
  • Don't rotate, skew, or recolor the logo
  • Don't stretch the lockup or alter its proportions
  • Don't substitute the typefaces ("just use Roboto")
  • Don't give all three accents equal weight
  • Don't set body text in Inter or Mono
  • Don't use exclamation points in product or marketing copy
  • Don't use Ion or Amber for body text on white (contrast fails)
  • Don't add glows, shadows, or filters — they're already baked in
